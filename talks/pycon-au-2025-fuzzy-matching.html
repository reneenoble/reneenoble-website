What's in a Name? Fuzzy Matching Techniques for Proper Nouns

PyCon AU 2025 Python Data NLP

About This Talk

Names are messy in customer databases, research data and government records. Typos, nicknames, transliterations and cultural variations all make exact matching fail.

This talk covers practical techniques for fuzzy matching names and proper nouns in Python, including string distance metrics and methods that account for common variations in personal names.
