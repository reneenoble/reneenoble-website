How Smart is AI? Real projects to build real understanding

PyCon AU 2024 Python AI Education

About This Talk

AI is everywhere, but its capabilities are often poorly understood. This talk uses practical projects to test what AI can and cannot do without relying on the hype.

Perfect for educators looking for ways to teach AI concepts, or anyone wanting to better understand the technology that's shaping our world.
