Four-part Python series

What in the World-le

Build a word game, use data to choose a first word, write a solver and experiment with an AI player.

About the series

This series uses a familiar word game to explore progressively richer Python ideas. You will begin with strings, loops, files and terminal output, then move into Jupyter notebooks, visualisation, search strategies and natural language processing.

What you will build

A playable terminal game, a data-driven first-word analysis, a rule-based solver and an experimental AI player.
